self-driving lab (SDL) workshop. It is a living list — please open a PR to add,
remove, or update entries as planning progresses.

## Local / Regional Options

Smaller, lower-overhead venues that could host a standalone workshop near
collaborating institutions:

- University of Wisconsin–Madison
- Brigham Young University (Provo, UT)
- University of Utah (Salt Lake City, UT)
- New York, NY (near Radical AI)

## Selection Criteria (to evaluate each option)

When comparing the options above, consider:

- Audience fit (structural alloys + self-driving labs / autonomous experimentation)
- Date and lead time relative to project milestones
- Travel cost and accessibility for likely attendees
- Cost and availability of suitable meeting space
- Co-location with collaborator institutions
