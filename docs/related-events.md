# Related Workshops, Symposia, and Events

This document catalogs workshops, symposia, and events — both **prior** and
**future** — that are topically related to the structural-alloy self-driving
lab (SDL) workshop. The goal is to:

- Avoid duplicating effort already covered by previous events.
- Identify communities and audiences to engage.
- Spot scheduling opportunities (co-location, back-to-back) and conflicts.
- Surface organizers, speakers, and materials we can learn from.

This is a living list — please open a PR to add, correct, or update entries.
For the broader list of candidate *venues* for hosting our own workshop, see
[issue #5](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/structural-alloy-sdl-workshop/issues/5)
(tracked in [PR #6](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/structural-alloy-sdl-workshop/pull/6)).
The conferences from issue #5 (Accelerate, TMS, MS&T, MRS) are included here
as well, but this list is intentionally **not** restricted to that set.

## How entries are organized

Events are grouped by topical focus and then sorted roughly chronologically
(most recent / upcoming first within each group). Each entry notes:

- **When/where** — date and location (or "annual" / "recurring" if it is a
  series).
- **Focus** — what the event is about and why it is relevant.
- **Link** — official site, call for papers, or a representative past edition.

Where a precise date is not yet announced, an approximate window is given.
Please verify dates against the official event site before relying on them.

## Self-driving labs and autonomous experimentation

These events are the closest topical match — autonomous / self-driving labs,
materials acceleration platforms (MAPs), and closed-loop experimentation.

### Conferences and recurring meetings

- **Accelerate Conference** — Annual, hosted by the
  [Acceleration Consortium](https://acceleration.utoronto.ca/) at the
  University of Toronto. The flagship gathering of the self-driving-labs
  community; explicit overlap with our audience. Next edition expected
  ~June 2027 in Toronto. See
  <https://acceleration.utoronto.ca/accelerate-conference>.
- **Autonomous Discovery Symposium** — Recurring symposium organized in
  partnership with Argonne National Laboratory and collaborators, focused on
  autonomous discovery across chemistry, materials, and biology. Past
  editions in 2021, 2023, and 2024. See
  <https://autonomous-discovery.lbl.gov/> and
  <https://www.anl.gov/event/autonomous-discovery-symposium>.
- **Acceleration Consortium training schools and workshops** — Recurring
  short courses and hands-on workshops on Bayesian optimization, SDL
  hardware/software, and lab automation. See
  <https://acceleration.utoronto.ca/events>.

### Recent workshops at major ML / AI venues

- **AI for Accelerated Materials Design (AI4Mat)** — Annual workshop at
  NeurIPS since 2022 (also held at ICLR). Strong overlap with the ML side of
  SDLs; many SDL groups present here. See
  <https://sites.google.com/view/ai4mat>.
- **Machine Learning for Materials (ML4Materials)** — Workshop series at
  ICLR / NeurIPS focused on ML methods for materials science, including
  active learning and autonomous experimentation. See
  <https://www.ml4materials.com/>.
- **AI for Science** — Recurring workshop at NeurIPS / ICML covering
  closed-loop experimentation as a sub-theme.
- **Differentiable Almost Everything / SyntheticData4ML / Adaptive
  Experimental Design** — Adjacent NeurIPS / ICML workshops that frequently
  feature SDL-relevant methods (Bayesian optimization, active learning,
  experimental design).

### MRS / TMS / MS&T symposia on autonomous experimentation

These are symposia *within* the larger conferences listed under "Materials
science conferences" below, and are particularly relevant:

- **MRS — Symposia on autonomous experimentation and machine learning for
  materials** — Recurring at both Spring and Fall MRS Meetings (recent
  examples: "Autonomous Experimentation in Materials Research", "Machine
  Learning and Autonomous Methods in Materials Science"). See
  <https://www.mrs.org/meetings-events>.
- **TMS — Symposia on AI/ML and autonomous experimentation in alloy
  design** — Recurring at the TMS Annual Meeting; particularly relevant
  because the audience overlaps with structural alloys. See
  <https://www.tms.org/AnnualMeeting>.
- **MS&T — Symposia on data science, ML, and high-throughput methods for
  materials** — Recurring at MS&T. See <https://www.matscitech.org/>.

## Structural alloys (and high-throughput / ICME methods)

Events focused on the *materials* side of the workshop — alloy design,
high-throughput experimentation, and integrated computational materials
engineering (ICME) — where SDL methods are increasingly relevant.

- **TMS Annual Meeting & Exhibition** — Annual (spring), rotates in the US.
  The flagship structural-alloys audience; many relevant symposia (alloy
  design, additive manufacturing, ICME, AI/ML). See
  <https://www.tms.org/AnnualMeeting>.
- **International Symposium on Superalloys** — Quadrennial (next expected
  ~2028), Seven Springs, PA. The premier event for high-temperature
  structural alloys. See <https://www.tms.org/Superalloys>.
- **MS&T (Materials Science & Technology)** — Annual (fall), rotates in the
  US. Joint conference of ACerS / AIST / ASM / TMS. See
  <https://www.matscitech.org/>.
- **MRS Spring and Fall Meetings** — Biannual, rotates in the US. Symposium
  format — many relevant individual symposia each year. See
  <https://www.mrs.org/meetings-events>.
- **World Congress on Integrated Computational Materials Engineering
  (ICME)** — Recurring (typically biennial). Directly relevant to closing
  the loop between simulation, experiment, and design. See
  <https://www.tms.org/ICME>.
- **International Conference on High-Throughput Vapor Deposition (HTVD)**
  and related combinatorial-materials workshops — Smaller, focused events
  on high-throughput experimentation methods that pair naturally with SDLs.

## Lab automation, robotics, and chemistry SDLs

Adjacent communities where many SDL techniques originated; useful for
methods and tooling even though the materials focus differs.

- **SLAS (Society for Laboratory Automation and Screening) International
  Conference and Exhibition** — Annual (typically February). The largest
  lab-automation event; strong vendor presence relevant to SDL hardware.
  See <https://www.slas.org/events/>.
- **Festival of Biologix / LabAutomation Europe** — Recurring European
  lab-automation events.
- **ACS (American Chemical Society) symposia on autonomous chemistry and
  high-throughput experimentation** — Recurring at ACS National Meetings
  (spring and fall). See <https://www.acs.org/meetings.html>.
- **NeurIPS / ICML Robot Learning workshops** — Adjacent to the robotics
  half of SDLs.

## Notable past workshops worth reviewing

Concrete prior workshops whose scope, format, or materials are useful
reference points when designing ours. (This list is intentionally short and
representative — not exhaustive.)

- **AI4Mat @ NeurIPS 2022 / 2023 / 2024** — Format, topics, and accepted
  papers are a good reference for the ML-facing portion of an SDL workshop.
  <https://sites.google.com/view/ai4mat>.
- **Autonomous Discovery Symposium (2021, 2023, 2024)** — Multi-day,
  cross-domain (chemistry / materials / biology) program; useful template
  for a broader SDL agenda.
- **Acceleration Consortium "Self-Driving Labs" summer schools** —
  Hands-on, training-oriented format; useful template if we want a
  tutorial / hackathon component.
- **MRS symposia on "Autonomous Experimentation in Materials Research"**
  (multiple recent editions) — Closest prior art for an SDL-focused
  symposium embedded in a major materials conference.
