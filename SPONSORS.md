# Potential Sponsors

This document tracks organizations that may be a good fit to sponsor the
**Structural Alloy Self-Driving Lab (SDL) Workshop** hosted by the
Vertical Cloud Lab @ BYU. It is a working list intended to support
outreach planning; inclusion here does not imply endorsement by, or any
existing relationship with, the listed organizations.

Sponsors are grouped by the type of value they could bring to the
workshop (funding, materials, instrumentation, compute, software, or
community reach). For each candidate we capture a short rationale for
why they may be interested in a structural-alloy SDL workshop.

## How to use this list

- Treat each entry as a starting point for research, not a confirmed
  contact.
- Before reaching out, verify current sponsorship programs and points
  of contact on the organization's website.
- When a sponsor commits, move them to an `Acknowledged Sponsors`
  section (to be added) and remove them from the candidate list below.
- New suggestions are welcome via pull request; please follow the
  format used here and include a one-line rationale.

## Evaluation criteria

When deciding whether to approach a candidate sponsor, consider:

1. **Topical alignment** — does the organization work on structural
   alloys, additive manufacturing, autonomous experimentation, or
   materials informatics?
2. **Audience alignment** — does the workshop audience (graduate
   students, early-career researchers, and industry practitioners in
   materials science and cloud-enabled experimentation) match the
   organization's recruiting or outreach goals?
3. **Type of contribution** — funding, hardware loans, sample
   materials, cloud credits, software licenses, travel grants for
   participants, or speakers.
4. **Prior engagement** — existing relationships with BYU, the
   Vertical Cloud Lab, or other SDL community efforts.

## Candidate sponsors

### Government and non-profit funding agencies

- **U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF)** — programs such as DMREF,
  CSSI, and the Materials Research portfolio routinely support
  workshops on autonomous experimentation and materials informatics.
- **U.S. Department of Energy (DOE), Office of Science** — Basic
  Energy Sciences and the Advanced Manufacturing Office both fund
  structural-materials and self-driving-lab efforts.
- **U.S. Department of Defense research offices** (ARO, AFOSR, ONR) —
  long-standing interest in high-performance structural alloys for
  aerospace and defense applications.
- **NASA** — interest in lightweight and high-temperature structural
  alloys for aerospace platforms.
- **National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)** — Material
  Measurement Laboratory and the Materials Genome Initiative align
  closely with SDL workshop topics.
- **The Minerals, Metals & Materials Society (TMS)** — professional
  society that frequently co-sponsors workshops and short courses.
- **ASM International** — materials-focused professional society with
  education and outreach programs.

### Industry — alloy producers and metals manufacturers

- **ATI (Allegheny Technologies Inc.)** — specialty alloys for
  aerospace, defense, and energy.
- **Carpenter Technology** — specialty alloys and additive
  manufacturing powders.
- **Haynes International** — high-performance nickel- and
  cobalt-based alloys.
- **Special Metals (PCC)** — nickel-based superalloys.
- **Kennametal** — tooling and wear-resistant alloy systems.
- **Alcoa** and **Arconic** — aluminum alloys for structural
  applications.
- **Boeing**, **Lockheed Martin**, **Northrop Grumman**, **GE
  Aerospace**, **Pratt & Whitney**, **Rolls-Royce** — end users of
  advanced structural alloys with active university outreach
  programs.

### Industry — additive manufacturing and instrumentation

- **EOS**, **SLM Solutions (Nikon)**, **Velo3D**, **3D Systems**,
  **Desktop Metal**, **Markforged** — metal additive-manufacturing
  systems often used in alloy SDL setups.
- **Renishaw** — metrology and additive manufacturing.
- **Thermo Fisher Scientific**, **Bruker**, **Zeiss**, **JEOL**,
  **Hitachi High-Tech**, **Oxford Instruments** — characterization
  instrumentation (SEM, EBSD, XRD, EDS) frequently used in
  structural-alloy SDL workflows.
- **Instron**, **MTS Systems**, **Zwick Roell** — mechanical-testing
  equipment vendors.

### AI-for-science labs and companies

### Local and regional partners
