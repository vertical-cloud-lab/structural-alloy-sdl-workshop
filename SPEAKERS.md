## Selection Criteria

Candidates were prioritized using the following criteria:

1. **Topical fit** — direct work on self-driving labs *and/or* structural
   metallic alloys (ferrous, Ni-base superalloys, Ti alloys, refractory
   alloys, high-entropy alloys, lightweight alloys).
2. **Methods breadth** — coverage of synthesis, characterization, modeling,
   ML/Bayesian optimization, and orchestration.
3. **Sector mix** — balance across academia, national labs, and industry.
4. **Diversity** — institutional, geographic, career-stage, and demographic
   diversity across the final invited slate.
5. **Availability signal** — recent talks at related venues (MRS, TMS,
   Acceleration Consortium, Faraday Discussions, Gordon Conferences) suggest
   active engagement with this community.
