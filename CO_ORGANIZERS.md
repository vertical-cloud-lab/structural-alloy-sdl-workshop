# Potential Co-Organizers

This document captures candidate co-organizers for the **Structural Alloy SDL
(Self-Driving Lab) Workshop**. Candidates are organized into three categories
— academic, national lab, and industry — to ensure the program reflects the
full structural-alloy autonomous-experimentation ecosystem (alloy design,
processing, characterization, mechanical testing, and ICME/ML integration).

The lists below are starting points for outreach, not commitments. Add,
remove, or annotate entries as conversations progress. For each contact,
please record:

- Name and affiliation
- Role / relevant expertise (e.g., autonomous synthesis, high-throughput
  mechanical testing, alloy informatics, AI/ML for materials)
- Status of outreach (not contacted, contacted, confirmed, declined)
- Notes (availability, prior workshops, conflicts of interest)

## Diversity and balance considerations

When finalizing the co-organizer slate, aim for balance across:

- Sector (academic / national lab / industry roughly equally represented)
- Career stage (early-career, mid-career, senior)
- Demographic diversity and broadening participation
- Geographic distribution (U.S. regions plus international partners)
- Technical scope (alloy design, processing, characterization,
  mechanical testing, automation/robotics, ML/informatics)
